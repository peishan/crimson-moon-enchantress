## Contradictions with the script (beyond numbering)

1. **#2, #3, #5 invent a different first meeting.** In the art Xiyu and Dongyue meet in the forest at night. In the script they first meet at the Crimson Fairy (004), and #6 then has her introduce herself as if for the first time. The two cannot both stand.
2. **Hair continuity.** The script says Dongyue *always* has long silvery-white hair. He is black-haired in #2 and #3.
3. **Xiyu undressing / "naked".** Script 006 has her wrap in a *light inner garment* before going to the stream. Art #7 shows her slipping out of her garments, and the narration in #1, #7, #8 says "naked". This also breaks the author's own rule (inner garments or sleepwear when she cools down).
4. **Script 008 line missing.** "Then let me be afraid beside you" appears in neither version. #9 has "Try me" instead; #10 drops the exchange.
5. **Extra facts not in the script** (confirm or cut): Xiyu is eighteen (#1); Dongyue's home is "Sanggui, ten thousand li from Yilong" (#4); the ship's compass jumps from northeast to southwest (#4); Muxue Yan is "a wise swordsman" who discovered the island (#6); Xiao Tian already mentions the woman in grey in #7 (the script first has this in ch 14).
