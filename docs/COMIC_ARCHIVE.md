# Comic Chapter 001 --- "The Witch Beneath Eight Moons"

**Status:** Summary recovered; exact panel dialogue and narration
unavailable verbatim.

A five-scene opening introduces Muxue Island and Muxue Xiyu, establishes
the Blood Moon prophecy and the cost of her magic, then follows her
grief over Xu Hanshan's departure, an unnatural earthquake, and her
decision to investigate the disturbance.

### Scene beats

1.  **The witch of Muxue:** Establish the isolated magical island and
    Xiyu's striking, icy presence.
2.  **The Blood Moon legacy:** Narration introduces the prophecy that
    Xiyu was born beneath the Blood Moon and inherited the power of the
    eclipse, with sensual side effects when she uses or practises magic.
3.  **Abandoned:** Xiyu remembers the loss of her parents and Xu
    Hanshan's departure. Her pain is framed as grief over losing her
    only close companion, not necessarily romantic heartbreak.
4.  **The tremor:** An unnatural earthquake interrupts her solitude
    beneath the eight moons. Xiyu takes shelter and realises her
    offensive magic does not automatically protect her from natural
    danger.
5.  **Veiled departure:** Xiyu dresses, veils herself to avoid
    recognition, and heads into the forest to discover what disturbed
    the island's balance.

**Visual notes:** Use clear vertical-scroll spacing and readable text.
Keep the Chinese fantasy aesthetic and moonlit palette. Avoid
overcrowded panels; use narration for internal thoughts when needed.

# Comic Chapter 005 --- "The Crimson Fairy"

**Status:** Summary recovered; exact panel dialogue and narration
unavailable verbatim.

Dongyue and his companions meet Long Ra'ya, the village mayor, and A'Bu,
the austere bell-bearing elder. They learn basic lore about Muxue and
are warned about the powerful witch of the Muxue lineage. The travellers
are brought to the Crimson Fairy, where Dongyue meets its veiled
proprietress, Muxue Xiyu.

### Key beats

1.  **Meeting the mayor:** Long Ra'ya introduces himself and discovers
    that Yilong is unknown to the people of Muxue.
2.  **A'Bu's warning:** The elder explains that Muxue cannot be reached
    by just anyone and describes the island's strange history and
    customs.
3.  **The witch's inn:** The party arrives at the Crimson Fairy, a
    light-red inn with a brown roof and small red lanterns.
4.  **First meeting:** Xiyu introduces herself as the proprietress. Her
    pink veil conceals the lower half of her face, while her eyes
    captivate Dongyue.
5.  **A new curiosity:** Dongyue finds himself unusually fascinated by
    Xiyu, while the people around him fear her reputation.

**Continuity:** Dongyue's hair is silvery-white; Xiyu is veiled at their
first meeting; preserve the Chinese fantasy design and red-toned inn
exterior.

# Comic Chapter 019 --- "Xiyu's Thoughts"

**Status:** Summary recovered; exact panel dialogue and narration
unavailable verbatim.

During the journey, Xiyu reflects on her childhood and her mother Lady
Peony's words: "Xiaoxi, you are the last of the Muxue clan. Do us
proud." She remembers being chosen by magic, her devotion to the dark
moon deity Minami, her granduncle Fangyi's diary, and her repeated
failed attempts to reach the portal in the mountains.

### Key beats

1.  **A childhood memory:** Young Xiyu remembers her mother's
    instruction to do the Muxue clan proud.
2.  **Chosen by magic:** Narration explains how Xiyu chose enchantment
    and offensive magic, and why she sees protecting the island as her
    responsibility.
3.  **The hidden inheritance:** Fangyi's diary revealed clues about the
    mountains, the portal, and the chest she believes is her birthright.
4.  **A deliberate plan:** Xiyu admits to herself that she recruited
    Dongyue partly because she needed help reaching the hidden
    destination.
5.  **Fear of abandonment:** Despite her growing feelings, she expects
    Dongyue to leave her eventually, just as Hanshan did.
6.  **The unanswered choice:** She wonders whether she wants greater
    power to protect Muxue, freedom from her curse, or the chance to
    experience love without fear.

**Emotional direction:** Contrast Xiyu's outwardly icy persona with her
private loneliness and uncertainty. Use narration and visual memories
rather than packing every thought into dialogue.

# Character and continuity reference

## Muxue Xiyu --- 慕雪细雨

-   **Role:** Moon witch / enchantress; proprietress of the Crimson
    Fairy.
-   **Visual identity:** Cold gaze, pale complexion, long dark hair,
    veiled lower face in public, Chinese fantasy clothing.
-   **Magic:** Enchantment and offensive spells, including fire-based
    attacks.
-   **Character contrast:** Outwardly icy and ruthless; privately lonely
    and afraid of abandonment.

## Zhan Dongyue --- 展东悦

-   **Role:** Fighter with healing / cleric-like abilities; explorer and
    trading-ship captain.
-   **Visual identity:** **Long silvery-white hair. This must remain
    consistent.**
-   **Character traits:** Curious, calm under pressure, persistent,
    compassionate, and inexperienced in romantic relationships.

## Ya'Xing --- 亚星

-   **Role:** Ship and practical support; helps with the vessel and
    group logistics.
-   **Character traits:** More easily alarmed by danger than Dongyue.

## Xiao Tian --- 萧天 (provisional)

-   **Role:** Martial companion with amnesia; loyal sworn brother to
    Dongyue.
-   **Story thread:** Dreams of a woman in grey who may be his mother.

## Muxue Yan --- 慕雪岩 (provisional)

-   **Role:** Ancient Muxue ancestor and imprisoned sage.
-   **Visual continuity:** Preserve the appearance used in the
    approved/referenced design; the exact image is not embedded in this
    Markdown archive.

## Other important names

-   **Xu Hanshan:** Xiyu's childhood companion.
-   **Wang Xi'er:** Hanshan's wife.
-   **Long Ra'ya:** Mayor of the village.
-   **A'Bu:** Bell-bearing elder.
-   **Pei Xinyi:** Xiyu's distant cousin.

**Name note:** Chinese character forms marked "provisional" should be
confirmed before being treated as final canon. Keep the author's
preferred spellings consistent across future assets.

# Adaptation standards for future comic chapters

-   Use a **vertical-scroll** layout suitable for phone screens.
-   Prefer fewer, clearer panels when a conversation becomes visually
    dense.
-   Shorten dialogue where appropriate and use narration for implied
    thoughts.
-   Keep text large enough to read on a phone; avoid blurry or crowded
    lettering.
-   Avoid duplicated characters within a panel unless the scene
    intentionally depicts movement or a sequence.
-   Preserve established character appearances, especially Dongyue's
    silvery-white hair and Muxue Yan's design.
-   Keep all art direction rooted in Chinese / Eastern xianxia fantasy.
    Avoid Western angelic motifs.
-   When Xiyu cools down because of her magic, depict appropriate inner
    garments or sleepwear.
-   Label each instalment with its **comic chapter number and comic
    title**, independently of the original book chapter number.
