# The Long Awaited Love Is Not a Dream

## Game Adaptation --- Short-Form Comic / Visual-Novel Script

**Pen name:** Yuchen Sky

> **Purpose:** This is the shortened adaptation for a game/comic format,
> not the original long book chapters. Narration and dialogue have been
> condensed for mobile readability and faster pacing. Where an earlier
> exact script was not recoverable from the session record, this version
> is a clean, newly assembled adaptation from the available story
> material---not a claim to reproduce previous wording verbatim.

## Style and continuity

-   Vertical-scroll, phone-friendly scenes with short dialogue boxes.
-   Use narration for internal thoughts and transitions; avoid panels
    crowded with dialogue.
-   Chinese / Eastern xianxia fantasy only; no Western angelic styling.
-   **Zhan Dongyue always has long silvery-white hair.**
-   Xiyu has long dark hair and often wears a veil in public. When
    resting or cooling down from her magic, show suitable inner garments
    or sleepwear.
-   Avoid duplicated characters in a panel. Keep Muxue Yan's approved
    appearance consistent.

# Comic 001 --- The Witch Beneath Eight Moons

**Adapted from Book Chapter 1: "Return of the Witch"**

### Scene 1 --- The Blood Moon

**Narration:** On Muxue, an island hidden from the world, a child was
born beneath the Blood Moon. The power of the eclipse chose Muxue
Xiyu---and left her with a curse of its own.

**Xiyu (thought):** "Power comes easily. Peace never does."

### Scene 2 --- Left Behind

**Narration:** Her parents were gone. Now Xu Hanshan, her childhood
companion of seven years, had left her too.

**Xiyu:** "Was being my friend really so frightening?"

### Scene 3 --- The Tremor

The earth suddenly shakes. Xiyu grabs an oak tree as the forest quakes
around her.

**Xiyu:** "This isn't natural..."

### Scene 4 --- The Veil

Xiyu dresses, covers her face, and heads into the forest.

**Narration:** She had hidden from the island for two years. But if
Muxue was in danger, she could not hide any longer.

**Xiyu:** "Whatever disturbed this island, I'll find it."

# Comic 002 --- The Intruders of Muxue

**Adapted from Book Chapter 2: "Welcome to Muxue"**

### Scene 1 --- A Violent Sea

A trading vessel bucks across strangely turbulent water. Dongyue,
silvery hair tied back, calmly reads his charts.

**Ya'Xing:** "Young Master! The water is pulling us in!"

**Dongyue:** "There's no storm. Look carefully."

### Scene 2 --- Three Men, Three Fates

Xiao Tian clasps his hands in silent prayer. Ya'Xing clings to the mast
as the vessel spins.

**Ya'Xing:** "I knew this journey would kill me!"

### Scene 3 --- A World of Snow

The chaos stops. Blue water and clear skies vanish, replaced by snow and
unfamiliar land.

**Dongyue:** "Snow... so this place is real."

He leaps ashore and tastes the snow, delighted.

### Scene 4 --- Welcome

A crowd gathers around the stranded vessel.

**Elder:** "Welcome to the island of Muxue, young travellers."

**Narration:** None of their maps had ever shown this island. None of
them knew how they had arrived.

# Comic 003 --- The First Encounter

**Adapted from the arrival sequence**

### Scene 1 --- The Mayor

Long Ra'ya, the village mayor, greets the strangers.

**Dongyue:** "I am Zhan Dongyue, a merchant from Yilong."

**Long Ra'ya:** "Yilong? I'm afraid none of us knows that name."

### Scene 2 --- The Unreachable Island

A stern elder named A'Bu explains that Muxue cannot be reached by
ordinary means.

**A'Bu:** "Someone among you must have had the affinity to cross our
boundary."

### Scene 3 --- Eight Moons

Dongyue listens as the island's strange history unfolds: one sun, eight
moons, snow through every season, and magic woven into daily life.

**Dongyue (thought):** "A world outside every map. I must learn
everything I can."

### Scene 4 --- A Warning

**A'Bu:** "If you meet the last witch of the Muxue line, keep your
distance."

Dongyue smiles, more curious than afraid.

**Dongyue:** "Now I certainly want to meet her."

# Comic 004 --- Welcome to Muxue

The travellers approach a red-painted inn beneath the snow.

**Long Ra'ya:** "You'll stay here. Its proprietress is... unusual."

The mayor warns them that the innkeeper is a powerful, cursed witch who

**Ya'Xing:** "A cursed witch? And we're staying with her?"

### Scene 3 --- A Veiled Figure

A young woman approaches with a pot of steaming porridge. A sheer pink
veil hides the lower half of her face.

**Xiyu:** "I am Muxue Xiyu, owner of the Crimson Fairy. You're the
travellers who arrived today?"

**Dongyue:** "Zhan Dongyue. Captain and owner of the vessel."

### Scene 4 --- The Eyes

Their eyes meet. Dongyue forgets what he meant to say.

**Dongyue (thought):** "What a beautiful name..."

**Narration:** For the first time in his life, a woman had captured his
attention.

# Comic 010 --- Helping Hanshan on a Condition

**Adapted from Book Chapter 8**

### Scene 1 --- A Desperate Visitor

Xu Hanshan arrives at the Crimson Fairy, exhausted and frightened.

**Hanshan:** "I must see Miss Muxue. My wife may not survive without her
help."

### Scene 2 --- The Past Returns

Xiyu receives him in the upper hall, keeping her distance.

**Xiyu:** "Our friendship ended, Hanshan. Why should I help you?"

**Hanshan:** "Because you're the only person I can ask."

### Scene 3 --- An Offer

Xiyu explains that she is an enchantress, not a healer. Dongyue steps
forward.

**Dongyue:** "I may be able to help."

### Scene 4 --- The Condition

Dongyue sits beside Xiyu and takes her hand.

**Dongyue:** "I'll help your wife---but you must never return to Xiyu's
life."

Xiyu looks at him, unable to decide whether to be angry or grateful.

# Comic 017 --- The Prisoner

**Dongyue:** "Xiyu! Stay with me!"

# Comic 020 --- A Deep Bond

**Adapted from Book Chapter 19**

### Scene 1 --- The New Distance

Xiyu hides her emotions behind her familiar icy expression.

**Xiyu:** "You healed me. How?"

**Dongyue:** "I don't fully understand it myself. I could heal a wounded
pigeon as a child. Later, I healed others. I've always hidden it."

### Scene 2 --- The Scroll

Dongyue remembers finding the ancient scroll and reading its strange
words as if they were written for him. He used its spell because Xiyu
was losing too much blood.

**Dongyue (thought):** "I only knew I couldn't let her die."

### Scene 3 --- A Bond Neither Understands

Xiyu senses an unfamiliar closeness between them.

**Xiyu (thought):** "Why can I feel his emotions? What has changed
between us?"

She realises that the ancient spell may have bonded their souls.

### Scene 4 --- Fear and Gratitude

**Xiyu:** "You barely know me. What do you want in return?"

**Dongyue:** "Rest first. We can speak of that when you're stronger."

### Scene 5 --- The Unspoken Truth

Xiyu retreats to a corner, clutching a loose sheet from the scroll.

**Xiyu (thought):** "I should be angry with him. So why am I afraid of
losing him?"

Dongyue offers to stay nearby while she rests.

**Dongyue:** "Let me take care of you."

**Xiyu:** "Don't try anything foolish, Young Master Zhan."

The torch goes dark. Their new bond remains unexplained.
