# Late-game plan (author planning notes; nothing built)

Status: planning only. Chapters, volumes and the exact artifact participants are not decided. These notes do not change any existing chapter, flag or enemy.

## The artifact is the turning point
Before it, the party are travelling companions who have gradually become a family. After it, they share an extraordinary fate none of them originally chose. What they do with that fate stays their decision.

The royal revelations come **after** the artifact encounter, so the payoff lands harder.

### Two effects
1. **Immortality:** the participating members can no longer die by ordinary means or age normally.
2. **A shared bond:** lasting, but subtle, never a mind-link and never a mechanism that forces them to stay together. It connects them; it does not control them. Candidate expressions (pick a few, do not use all):
   - they recognise one another's spiritual presence;
   - they sense when another participant is in extreme danger;
   - their spiritual energies resonate when they fight together;
   - a common mark or signature is left in their spiritual essence.

"Participating members" means whoever is present at the artifact expedition; who that is stays open. Guests and family not present are unaffected.

## Revised sequence
| Stage | Development |
|---|---|
| Earlier volumes | The party builds relationships and individual identities. |
| Artifact expedition | The participating companions unexpectedly gain immortality and a shared connection. |
| After the artifact | They confront no longer belonging to ordinary mortal society. |
| Muxue Yan arc | The group's history and relationships deepen. |
| Liechen's royal revelation | He confronts his royal past and eventually renounces his claim. |
| Ya'Xing's tribal origins arc | Tuoba Jingheng returns during a new tribal mystery and reveals Ya'Xing as the lost eldest prince. |
| Final choices | The characters decide how to live; brotherhood and shared purpose outrank inherited obligations. |

## A defining emotional payoff
After the artifact the group disagrees about what to do. Some propose separating to protect their families or avoid suspicion; others, secluded bases. They realise **nobody is required to remain.** Hoko can return to Luiterra, Ya'Xing to his tribe, Liechen can reconsider his royal obligations, Qiumin can choose a different life. **Yet they return to one another.**

Everyone has an alternative: Liechen a kingdom, Ya'Xing a tribe and inheritance, Hoko family in another world. They stay together because they chose each other, not because the artifact binds them.

## Fits with existing canon and plans (check before building)
- Liechen's mother was a queen of a small kingdom and he left the palace by choice (Volume 14, comics 027-029): the royal arc is already seeded; "renounces his claim" matches "I left the palace because I wanted to live by my own choices".
- **Tuoba Jingheng** (named in Volume 9 comic 028) is the traveller whose face resembles Muxue Yan to Xiyu; the Ya'Xing link is new. The earlier Volume 9 notes left him unresolved, so this choice does not contradict them, but the resemblance to Muxue Yan then needs an answer of its own.
- Hoko's leave and return when Luiterra opens (docs/ROSTER_STRUCTURE.md) is the same beat.
- Ya'Xing's Skyshard and the Ice Temple past are unexplained and could connect to his tribal origins.
- Immortality must be reconciled with the existing mortal stakes: Xining's pregnancy and recovery path (recovery, birth, postpartum, child care), Yun He's seven-day clock, and Dongyue's family. Only **artifact participants** are affected, so Xining, Qiumin, Donglin and others can stay mortal unless the story decides otherwise; decide who is "participating" before writing it.
- Dongyue's relative magic-resistance rule (docs/MAGIC_RULES.md): immortality is "cannot die by ordinary means", not invulnerability; keep it relative in the same way (an extraordinary attack can still hurt).
- Approval system proposal (docs/APPROVAL_DESIGN.md): a chosen-family theme suits it.
