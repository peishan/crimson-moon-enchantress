# Story notes — SPOILERS (author's clarifications; not shown in the game)

These are for whoever writes later chapters and Volume 2. **Do not surface them in Volume 1**: the conventions say Xiyu's identity and Yan's intentions stay unresolved.

## Xiyu's origin (author, confirmed)
Xiyu is *supposed* to be a "descendant" of the Muxue clan, but in truth she was **frozen in time** and **raised by people who believed they were her parents, who turned out to be her descendants**.
- This is why Muxue Yan questions whether she is "truly his descendant" (ch28), why the hook list speaks of "her father" and "a father she barely knows" (ch36, ch39), why Yan "promises to care for his descendant" (ch35), and why Yunxi is "his lost daughter" (ch24).
- Taken from the hook list, not stated by the author: Yan is the father in question. **Not asserted, please confirm:** whether Xiyu is Muxue Yunxi (the "Yun'er" she hears at Ao Yun's casket in ch27).
- Consequence for existing text: Lady Peony ("you are the last of the Muxue clan") and the others who raised her believed in good faith that she was their descendant. Public-facing wording in the game ("the last descendant of the Muxue clan", from the cast sheet) is what the islanders believe, and stays as is.

## Rules for the game
- No in-game line may state or confirm this in Volume 1. Hooks such as `xiyu_questions_her_lineage` (ch28) and `xiyu_doubts_about_yan_persist` (ch39) stay open.
- When the reveal is written, add its hook then; do not pre-set it.
