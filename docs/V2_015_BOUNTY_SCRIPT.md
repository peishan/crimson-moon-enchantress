# Volume 2 · Comic 015 — The Bounty Beneath the Red Lantern (author's script, 7 panels, plus the bounty gameplay note)

Saved from the author's message. In the game this is internal chapter 54 (`scripts/script_v2.js` holds the text; the bounty is `scripts/bounties.js`).

**Panel 1 — The Notice Beneath the Lantern.** The group reaches a busy settlement near Muxue's eastern boundary; a crowd around a noticeboard beneath red lanterns. A local guard pins up a fresh notice. Guard: "Another ward has failed. Something is attacking travellers after sunset." Xiyu: "A corrupted ward, missing travellers, and a reward. How convenient." Dongyue: "We should learn what happened before accepting."

**Panel 2 — The Missing Travellers.** Three disappearances along an old road; one traveller returned, but cannot remember what happened after seeing a red light between the trees. Xining: "Has anyone examined the survivor?" Guard: "He refuses to leave his house. Says something followed him home." Xining: "Then the reward can wait. I want to see him first."

**Panel 3 — What the Body Remembers.** Xining checks his pulse; his hands tremble whenever the lantern flame flickers. Survivor: "It didn't wound me. It took something away." Xining: "Your body is exhausted, but your meridians aren't damaged in the usual way. Whatever he encountered affected his spiritual energy without leaving an ordinary injury." Dongyue's expression hardens; the description sounds too familiar.

**Panel 4 — Two Kinds of Magic.** Cangyuan traces golden threads around the damaged ward; Xiyu sends a thin crimson spell across it. The ward briefly reconstructs, then black filaments emerge. Cangyuan: "The corruption was already inside the formation." Xiyu: "So it wasn't broken from outside." Cangyuan: "Something changed its pattern before the ward failed." Their magic begins to interfere; Cangyuan catches Xiyu's wrist. "Slowly. Let me separate the threads first." Xiyu nods, suddenly more conscious of his hand than she wants to admit.

**Panel 5 — The Thing in the Road.** A shadow rises from beneath the broken lantern: no clear face, tangled black threads and a pulsing red core. Dongyue steps between it and Xining: "Stay behind me." Xining: "I can help without standing behind you." She throws a cleansing powder across its exposed core; it recoils long enough for Dongyue to strike. Xining: "Its outer layer reacts to spiritual energy. The core is vulnerable when exposed!" Cangyuan redirects the threads while Xiyu's crimson spell severs the corrupted anchor beneath the road.

**Panel 6 — The Reward Is Not the Answer.** The shadow collapses, leaving a small black-red fragment in the road. Xining wraps it without touching the residue. "The survivor's symptoms match the spiritual disturbance in this fragment." Dongyue: "Can you treat him?" Xining: "I can help his body recover. I can't promise that will restore what was taken." Cangyuan: "This was not an isolated incident. Someone, or something, is testing the island's wards."

**Panel 7 — The First Bounty.** The guard pays the reward. Guard: "You've earned it. Travellers can use the road again." Dongyue accepts the pouch, gaze on the forest: "For now." Xiyu glances at Cangyuan, studying the fragment, expression unreadable. Xiyu (thought): Every time we find an answer, another question appears. Far beneath the forest a black filament slips into a crack in the earth. Unknown Voice: "They have begun to follow the trail." END OF COMIC 015.

## Gameplay note — Bounty: The Red Lantern Road
Repeatable bounty / introductory combat quest. Unlock after Comic 014 recruitment events.
Objective sequence: accept the bounty from the eastern settlement's noticeboard; interview the survivor; inspect the damaged ward with Cangyuan's Spirit Weaving or Xiyu's crimson magic; defeat the shadow creature and destroy its corrupted anchor; return to the guard to claim the reward.
Contributions: Xiyu high magical damage, destroys the anchor; Cangyuan controls and redirects threads, reveals ward patterns; Dongyue frontline and precise strikes; Xining diagnoses spiritual damage, cleansing support and recovery.
Rewards: currency and ordinary crafting materials, a small amount of experience, `red_lantern_road_cleared = true`, one corrupted fragment. Optional objective: finish without letting the shadow drain a party member's spiritual energy (extra crafting material).
Repeatable version: after story completion, similar road disturbances generate smaller randomized bounties; the original survivor and mystery scenes do not replay.
Flags: `xining_recruited = true`, `red_lantern_bounty_available = true`, `red_lantern_road_cleared = false` (true only after reporting back), `corrupted_fragment_obtained = false`.
Continuity: Xining's healing stays grounded in medicine and diagnosis; she cannot automatically reverse spiritual corruption. Xiyu and Cangyuan's closeness builds gradually; Dongyue's attachment to Xining stays subtle, not a sudden romance.
