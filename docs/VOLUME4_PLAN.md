# Volume 4: planning notes (author decisions; NOT built and NOT on any page yet)

## Hoko: a temporary, story-required party member (settled)
- **Who:** Hoko, a western explorer of Luiterra. Freed in Volume 1 (comic 026: trapped about a month in the glass room; he led Xiyu and Dongyue to the teleportation platform and unlocked it with the white key). He is in the Cast as a person (`people.js`, intro chapter 26) with no hero sheet, skills or portrait yet.
- **Where he returns:** at the Ice Temple in Volume 4, where **Yan's men are protecting him** alongside the other people stationed there.
- **How he joins:** **automatically**, when the party meets him during the **Ya'Xing retrieval mission**. He takes a **temporary seat** for the relevant Ice Temple sequence (Ya'Xing remains the mission's main objective; the four protagonists remain the central cast). His knowledge of the temple, the **white key** and the **teleportation mechanisms** is why he comes.
- **How he leaves:** after the mission the player can **say goodbye**, or leave his future involvement open. He is **not** a permanent recruit, so the roster does not grow with a game-origin character.
- **Condition:** establish **Yan's current stance before Hoko joins**. His men protect Hoko, but we do not yet know why (concern for the people at the temple, his search for a way to resurrect his wife, or something else). Hoko can be met without resolving Yan's motives; the game must not state a reason until a page does.

## Build notes (when the retrieval chapters exist)
- **Guest seat:** the planned `G.guests` list (docs/COMPANIONS.md): shown beside the active party, fielded in battle while a required arc is in range, removed when the arc ends. Two Volume 4 uses so far (Hoko now, plus the later Yilong guests: the Miracle Healer's Student and Dongyue's grandfather). It does not use one of the three flex seats, so Xiao Tian, Ya'Xing (after his arc) and the Miracle Healer keep theirs.
- **Hoko needs:** a hero sheet (class, stats, a few skills drawn from what the pages show: scouting, books and paintings, temple mechanisms), a portrait (`assets/cast/people/hoko.webp` is already on the art list) and a departure scene with a goodbye choice.
- **Not decided:** his combat role, level (a guest fights at the party's level, like story guests today), and whether "leave it open" can bring him back in a later volume.
