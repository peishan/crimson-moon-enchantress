# Xiyu's Muxue Inheritance: planning (not built)

Status: proposed canon, noted for Volume 21 and later. Nothing is built or wired into the game yet.

**Core idea.** The manuals teach Xiyu to understand her power; the sword gives that power a form. Together they are the foundation of her personal cultivation journey. It should feel ancient, specialised and deliberately preserved for a future descendant, not a pile of powerful equipment.

## Origin (corrected canon)
The inheritance is an **ancestral Muxue legacy preserved by Xiyu's forefathers across generations**. It was NOT personally prepared or left by Muxue Yan. In Volume 1, comic 018 Muxue Yan explains the blood-responsive mechanism linking the chest and his chains; Xiyu releases the chains and opens the chest with her Muxue bloodline, so she qualifies as a descendant. The letter is from her forefathers, not necessarily from Yan. Yan knew the Ice Temple mechanism, but that does not mean he created the inheritance or knew everything in it: it may hold knowledge even he did not fully understand.

## Components
| Component | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Muxue Inheritance Codex 慕雪传承秘典 | Three manuals, preserved together because each serves a different stage |
| First manual 慕雪血脉根本篇 (Bloodline Foundation) | Understand her inherited constitution and control the energy within her. New ability: **Moonlit Perception 月照灵识**: senses nearby magical disturbances and formation structure; later energy signatures and weaknesses in constructs. Limits: not omniscient, cannot reveal identity or intent, cannot expose every illusion, strong concealment still fools her. Useful for the Ice Temple formations. |
| Second manual 绯月术篇 (Crimson Moon Arts) | Controlled techniques. **Moonveil 月纱** (crimson-silver protective layer, reduces magical impact, limited force), **Crimson Threads 赤月灵丝** (bind, restrain, redirect small objects, interfere with casting; strong foes break them), **Crimson Crescent 绯月斩** (crescent strike effective against barriers and formations; depends on control and energy). Gives her an identity of protection, restraint and precise attack without outshining the others. |
| Third manual 天承修行篇 (Celestial Cultivation Scripture) | The long-term manual: energy circulation, a stable foundation, advanced stages, and the relationship between spiritual power, longevity and mortal limits. New ability: **Celestial Convergence 天灵共鸣** (harmonise with compatible formations and spiritual sources; may help the group investigate ancient mechanisms and immortality). It does NOT contain a complete immortality method. |
| Sword **Feiyue Jian 绯月剑** (Crimson Moon Sword) | Elegant straight sword; silver-white blade with a fine crimson line that glows with spiritual energy; crescent-moon guard with celestial engravings; dark jade hilt with crimson detail; small red gem pommel; black lacquer scabbard with silver and crimson patterns. An heirloom, not an oversized battlefield sword. |
| Letter from her forefathers | Not a cultivation guide. (1) **Recognition** of the opener as a Muxue descendant; (2) **Responsibility**: the manuals and sword are tools entrusted to someone who must use them well; (3) **An unfinished path**: the ancestors admit their knowledge was incomplete and some questions about celestial power and spiritual existence were unanswered. Wording undecided (one ancestor or several generations). |
| Blood recognition mechanism | The mechanism that let Xiyu open the chest in Volume 1. |

The manuals and sword are complementary: the sword can be wielded without the manuals, but its deeper abilities stay locked; the manuals can teach cultivation, but the sword is the instrument through which she practises and expresses techniques.

## The sword's invincibility
Volume 1 calls it an invincible weapon. Canon reading: **the sword itself cannot be destroyed** by ordinary physical, elemental or magical means (not broken, melted, corroded, damaged by ordinary spiritual attacks or weakened by use). **The wielder is not invincible**: Xiyu can be hurt, disarmed, exhausted or defeated, and can meet foes she cannot beat just by holding it.

Three stages of awakening (one weapon, not three): **Blood Recognition 血脉认主** (accepts her bloodline), **Moonlight Edge 月华剑锋** (channels energy to extend reach and cut through magical constructs), **Celestial Release 天承解封** (disrupts powerful formations and barriers ordinary weapons cannot affect). No talking sword spirit for now; recognition shows as light, resonance and spiritual response. Whether it has a consciousness is a much later question.

## How Xiyu's power develops
1. **Recognition (Volume 21):** understands the first manual, begins Moonlit Perception, basic energy circulation; Feiyue recognises her bloodline.
2. **Control (future):** Moonveil and Crimson Threads; channels energy into Feiyue; finer control of her existing magic.
3. **Expression (future):** Crimson Crescent; Moonlight Edge; interacts with complex formations.
4. **Celestial cultivation (long-term):** studies the third manual in depth; Celestial Convergence; understands how her nature relates to longevity and immortality; contributes to the group's shared investigation.

Do not unlock everything when she opens the bundle.

## Volume 21, chapter 001 (suggested)
Keep the reveal intimate: Xiyu finally unwraps the inheritance she has carried since Volume 1; this time she reads. Cangyuan stays beside her. The first manual is about understanding her own foundation, not instantly becoming stronger. End as she opens the first page and finds a diagram she cannot yet understand, a reason to revisit the inheritance through the volume.

## Left deliberately open
Why Muxue Yan was connected to this inheritance for Xiyu, and how much he knew about her half-celestial nature (a possible later revelation that does not change the contents). Also: single ancestor or several generations wrote the letter; Muxue Yan's fate stays a late-game discovery (docs/LATE_GAME_PLAN.md).
