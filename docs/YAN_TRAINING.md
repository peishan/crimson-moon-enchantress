# Yan's Integrated Training System (Volume 7, working canon, author)

Not built yet. Recorded from the author's design update.

- **Yan, Apprentice Herbalist:** non-magical support; an ordinary practitioner, not an innate healer. Story scenes teach the basics (no grinding needed for the main plot); optional training raises proficiency and unlocks stronger remedies, camp benefits and extra dialogue. His remedies support recovery and stabilise minor problems; magical healing stays with gifted healers.
- **Training Ledger:** a dedicated menu. Each skill shows its mentor, proficiency (0-4), completed lessons and unlocked rewards. Four stages per skill: 1 Introduction (story or mentor lesson) · 2 Practice (optional task or repeated use) · 3 Field application (use in exploration or a quest) · 4 Proficiency (a skill challenge; advanced recipe, camp benefit or dialogue). Story events can advance the ledger automatically.
- **Mentors:** Xining herbalism and field medicine (1/4) · Xiyu cooking and preservation (1/4) · Cangyuan campcraft (1/4) · Dongyue observation and classification (1/4) · Xiao Tian wilderness discipline (0/4) · Ya'Xing gathering (0/4) · Hoko travel preparation (0/4). The starting values are examples, not final data.
- **Volume 7 pacing:** 001-005 ledger introduction and travel basics after the single-use portal · 006-011 the estate stay (wound care, cooking, campcraft, notes) · 012-016 Xiao Tian's Echo Recall; Yan maintains the room; Ya'Xing introduces gathering · 017-020 journey to the distant city; Hoko teaches route planning and travel kits · 021-025 the concealed repository; Yan helps assess mechanisms and preserve records, but does not solve Xiao Tian's family mystery · 026-030 a fuller support kit before the revenge confrontation; optional camp dialogue.
- **Long-term:** portable apothecary kit, better recipes, then a herb garden and apothecary at Xiyu's base with Yan maintaining supplies. The core revenge plot stays completable if every optional task is ignored.

## Conflict with what is built (needs the author's call)
Yan is currently built from the author's earlier character sheet as the **Wayfinder** class (mechanism operation, terrain, reconnaissance, structural support). This update makes him an **Apprentice Herbalist** (non-magical support). Both could fit if the herbalist training is the ledger layered on top (Wayfinder skills in battle, herbalism for camp and supplies), or his battle skills could be replaced with remedy-style support. The existing herbalism system (Xining's pouch, mortar and recipes) could host his crafting.

## Built (option 1: keep both)
Yan keeps the Wayfinder battle class; `scripts/ledger.js` adds the Training Ledger on his Party sheet (seven skills, mentors, four stages, starting values as in the design). Story advances come from `LEDGER_CH` (empty until Volume 7 chapters exist); optional practice (gathering, tavern meals) moves herbalism, cooking and gathering from stage 1 to 2 after 3 uses. Stage rewards are listed but only the ledger itself is built; effects on remedies, meals and camp are not wired yet. The ledger appears once Yan is in the party (Volume 7).
