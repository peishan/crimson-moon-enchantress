## Continuity safeguards
- Knowing Xiao Tian's father establishes a believable relationship between the families. It does NOT mean Yihai knows everything about Xiao Tian's past or solves the mountain-estate mystery early.
- His contacts must not conveniently expose **Ya'Xing's or Liechen's origins**; those stay reserved for much later.
