## Update: crew mechanics (author-agreed)
- **Permanent crew**: Shipmaster / Navigator, non-combat. Stays on the expedition even when the active fighting party changes; **does not occupy a combat slot** and cannot take part in battles. On land he is a supporting NPC, not a fighter. He can be endangered in an attack, but responds through seamanship (protecting the ship and crew), never by suddenly revealing hidden fighting ability.
- Ship functions: navigation (primary), maritime exploration (routes, currents, hazards), manoeuvres (evasion, docking), trade advice (cargo, ports, merchants).
- Family compatibility: he joins the travelling roster permanently but can spend downtime with Ming Yue in Yilong instead of joining every land expedition.
