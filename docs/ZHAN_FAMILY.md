## Inherited gifts (author decision: latent, not mastered)
Their mother's lineage gives each sibling a different, modest expression; their father is an ordinary human, so neither approaches Dongyue's exceptional celestial nature. Ming Yue heals the body, Donglin protects life, Qiumin seeks what lies beneath appearances.
- **Donglin, Protective Vitality** (defensive only): Vitality Sense (notices a failing body early), Steady Breath (regulates qi, resists exhaustion), Protective Pulse (briefly reinforces another's physical stability). Fits his surviving the attack on his father's ship. Not a healer.
- **Qiumin, Clear Insight** (spiritual discernment): Resonance Reading (senses whether a talisman, inscription or formation is active, damaged or incomplete), Lingering Impressions (occasionally recognises traces of powerful spiritual techniques), Clear Mind (resists confusion, mental interference and some illusions). It supports her scholarship; she still needs knowledge and research.
- **Volume 15:** neither demonstrates powers just because the potential exists. Donglin stays a merchant's son and martial escort; Qiumin a scholar, adventurer and capable martial practitioner. The abilities emerge gradually in later volumes.
- **In the game:** both are built with the latent skills **locked behind flags**: `donglin_protective_vitality` (Steady Breath, Protective Pulse) and `qiumin_clear_insight` (Clear Insight). No chapter sets either flag yet; set it in the chapter where the power wakes. Vitality Sense and Lingering Impressions are story abilities and are not built.
